Memorial Day: Honor veterans

May 24, 2023 at 3:00 a.m.

by From Staff Reports

Annette Beard/Pea Ridge TIMES file photograph

Members of the Pea Ridge Veterans of Foreign Wars have placed flags on the graves of veterans for many years. They will be placing American flags on graves of veterans at Pea Ridge Cemetery in recognition of Memorial Day at 8 a.m. Saturday, May 27. Everyone is welcome to participate.

Staff reports
